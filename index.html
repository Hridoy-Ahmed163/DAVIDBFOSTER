Our Story

A family line of faith.
A fresh call to the lost.

Faith was not discovered late. It was handed down — generation to generation — and it is still being lived out today.

David B. Foster Ministries exists to teach, encourage and equip. We believe the gospel is still the power of God, that faith is meant to be walked out in ordinary days, and that love is the most persuasive sermon a believer will ever preach.

Faith

We take God at His word and act on it — before the evidence arrives. Faith is not a feeling. It is a decision we keep making.

Love

Truth without love wounds. Love without truth wanders. We hold both, because Jesus did. People are never the enemy.

Victory

Questions about partnership

What exactly is Covenant Partnership?

It is the ministry's monthly giving family. Partners set an amount and give it each month, which lets us plan teaching, events and outreach instead of reacting month to month.

Do I have to give a set amount?

No. The tiers above are suggestions only. Any monthly amount — or a one-time gift — is received with gratitude. No one is turned away from a gathering for lack of giving.

Can I change or cancel my gift?

Yes. Contact the ministry at any time and we will adjust or cancel your monthly gift. There is no minimum commitment and no penalty for pausing.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Tax treatment depends on the ministry's registration and your location. Please contact the ministry for current giving and receipt details before relying on any deduction.

How else can I help if I cannot give monthly?

Pray. Share a teaching. Invite a friend to a gathering. Host a conversation. Give once. Every one of those moves the mission forward.
